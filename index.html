[Lật Lại] is a playground for Lục Bát.

*

This website lets you click, mix, and turn the rules of bằng–trắc— from words to shapes, and shapes back to words.

*

There's no right way to start. Just try something and see what happens.

What is [Lật lại]?

***

Lật Lại is a cross-platform project that revisits Lục Bát as a living system rather than a fixed poetic form. This website functions as the interactive extension of the project. Building on the physical zine and type specimen, the web tool allows users to engage directly with the rules of bằng–trắc through generative interactions. Users can compose Lục Bát by selecting words based on tone, or by assembling tonal shapes that later generate language.

Mickiliin (Le My Linh) is a young creative practicioner based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Her skillsets focus on [Editorial design], [Interaction design], [Motion design] and newest to the collection, [Type design].
